👩‍🔬 Welcome to BeanGPT!

Your AI-Powered Partner for Bean Breeding & Computational Biology

BeanGPT is a next-generation, domain-specialized large language model built to boost research in bean breeding and computational biology.

Trained on over 100 million words from a curated collection of more than 300,000 peer-reviewed papers — Web of Science, PubMed, OPCC field trials, technical reports, and multi-omics resources.

How Can You Use BeanGPT?

🔬 Explore Genes & Traits

Ask about genes, proteins, and multi-omics features.

📊 Analyze Field Trials

Query historical OPCC field trial summaries.

📚 Literature Reviews

Citation-backed syntheses on resistance & agronomy.

📈 Visualize & Predict

Trend visualization & environment predictions.
